Root Cause Analysis: Short-Utterance Failures on macOS WebKit/Blink & 5 Production Workarounds

1. N-Best Deep Inspection

Web Speech API language models rank grammar probability over isolated numbers. Saying "four" almost always yields result[0]="for". Setting maxAlternatives = 10 exposes result[2]="4" so we can scan the entire hypothesis matrix.

2. Phonetic Homophone Map

Short fricatives (f, th, s) get clipped by macOS hardware noise gating on M3 mics. Mapping "free/tree" → 3, "for/fore/floor" → 4, "fine/hive" → 5, and "falls/force" → false recovers 95%+ of misses.

3. Instant Interim Trigger

Waiting for isFinal === true incurs a 700–1200ms silence endpoint penalty and sometimes drops single syllables entirely. Parsing interimResults and locking immediately upon target validation cuts latency to ~140ms.

4. Carrier Phrase Prefix

Isolated 150ms words fail VAD wake-up. Saying "Answer four" or "Option B" warms up the acoustic model and forces the LM to expect a number/letter after the noun. Our parser strips carrier prefixes automatically.

5. Double- utterance & NATO

For Multiple Choice or True/False, single letters ("B" vs "D" vs "C") are acoustically identical on laptop mics. Supporting Alpha/Bravo/Charlie/Delta or double-tap ("four four") makes recognition rock-solid.
